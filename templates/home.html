Run the demo

  1. Scan the QR code with a phone, or open the link under it. You see only "Call 108" and the contact's number, with no medical data.
  2. Sign in as a verified responder (ID H-1001, PIN 1234) and scan again. The emergency card opens at once.
  3. Tap Request full history. Then open the demo inbox: the emergency contact has received a code and an approval link.
  4. Approve on the link, or type the code into the responder's screen. The full history unlocks for 30 minutes.
  5. Open the owner dashboard to see who looked, who approved, and every notification.
